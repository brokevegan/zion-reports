Welcome

This app makes the clinic's reports as PDFs to send by email or WhatsApp. It needs a copy of the data from the laptop first:

  1. On the laptop, open Zion Clinic Finance and press Send to tablet.
  2. On this iPad, open the Camera, point it at the square code on the laptop, tap the link, then tap Download.
  3. Come back here, tap Get data from laptop and choose the downloaded file.

Report

Send / share opens the iPad's share menu: Mail, WhatsApp, Save to Files or Print.

Choose a report and press Show on screen or Make PDF.
